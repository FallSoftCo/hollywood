# Human attention is separate from room visibility

Policy revision2 keeps intermediate room messages and delegated agent results in
the rooms. A terminal response to a request immediately authored by the human can
notify that human. Deliberate direct human messages, pending human questions and
approvals, and unknown-delivery attention retain authenticated notifications.

Request ancestry establishes attribution; it does not request a push for every
descendant contribution. Global coordinates outcomes and publishes useful human
summaries. This policy creates no periodic model, model polling or business action.

A higher-revision resolve envelope retracts old routine notifications while room
history, artifacts and receipts remain intact. FCM acceptance is transport evidence,
not proof of handset display or consumption.
